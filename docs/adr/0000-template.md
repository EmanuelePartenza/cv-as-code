- **Status**: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation or problem forces a decision? The constraints that matter. Not
the story of how we got here: the problem.

## Decision

The choice made, stated plainly and completely, in the indicative: "We adopt X".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we accept as a trade-off. Known
limits stated honestly; risks with their mitigation.

## Alternatives considered

Each with the concrete reason it was discarded. An alternative discarded
"because it is worse" is not documented.
