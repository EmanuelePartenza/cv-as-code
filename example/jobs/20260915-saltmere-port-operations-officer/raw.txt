Port Operations Officer
Port of Saltmere Harbour Authority

About the port

The Port of Saltmere handles the island ferry to Brumeval, a fishing fleet of
around forty vessels and small cargo traffic. The Harbour Authority is a team
of nine. We are looking for a Port Operations Officer to run the day-to-day
operation of the harbour and support the Harbour Master.

Responsibilities

- Coordinate vessel movements and berth allocation across the two basins
- Maintain the daily port log and produce the monthly operations report
- Act as the port's first point of contact for the coastguard and other
  emergency services
- Manage the harbour's stores, fuel supplies and contractor visits
- Keep the port's aids to navigation in good order with the maintenance
  contractor
- Deputise for the Harbour Master when required

Essential

- 3+ years in a marine operations environment
- GMDSS or VHF radio operator's certificate
- Experience coordinating with the coastguard or other emergency services
- Working knowledge of a port management system (berth planning, vessel
  scheduling)
- Strong written reporting skills

Desirable

- VTS operator certification
- Experience with vessel traffic monitoring
- French language skills, for our ferry link to Brumeval
- Experience maintaining aids to navigation

Terms

Salary £36,000 – £40,000 depending on experience. Days, with an on-call rota
one week in four. Applicants must provide a reference from their current
employer with their application.
