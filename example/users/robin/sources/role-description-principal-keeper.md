# Northern Isles Lights Board — role description

**Post:** Principal Keeper, crewed station
**Grade:** Keeper 3 · **Reports to:** District Superintendent
**Issued:** March 2017 (fictional document of the example user)

## Purpose of the post

The Principal Keeper is responsible for the safe and continuous exhibition
of the light and the sounding of the fog signal at a crewed station, for
the station's plant and buildings, and for the relief keepers on the
station's rota.

## Duties

1. Operate and maintain the optic, the lens drive, the lamp changer and the
   standby generating plant to the Board's schedules; record every defect
   and every test run in the station logbook.
2. Take and transmit the synoptic weather observations required by the
   national weather service at the station's assigned intervals.
3. Plan and manage the supply landings with the supply vessel's master and
   the district office; keep the station's inventory of consumables and
   spares within budget.
4. Hold the station's VHF watch and act as the first point of contact for
   the coastguard for incidents within sight of the station.
5. Induct and train relief keepers to the Board's competence standard; keep
   the station handbook current.
6. Host and supervise contractors and the Board's engineers on site.

## Systems and standards

The Board's stations use the LBMS maintenance log (paper at crewed
stations), the national weather service's SYNOP observation format, and
the Board's Keeper Competence Standard (KCS-2016).
