# Letter — National Weather Service, Observations Network

**From:** Observations Network Manager, National Weather Service (fictional)
**To:** R. Ashcombe, Principal Keeper, Skerra Head Lighthouse
**Date:** 3 March 2025

*(Copy kept by the keeper. Fictional document of the example user.)*

Dear Mr Ashcombe,

Thank you for your continued contribution to the observations network. Our
records show that Skerra Head has submitted its three-hourly synoptic
observations without a gap since April 2018, which makes it the only
crewed station in the northern district with an unbroken series over that
period.

We also note the volunteer project you proposed in January 2022 and have
run since then: the automated logging of the station's barometer and wind
readings at one-minute intervals, from an instrument shelter you built
yourself, with a monthly export sent to our climate section. The climate
section has used that series in two published reports on winter storms
(2023 and 2024), and would like to keep receiving it.

Should the station be automated, we would be glad to discuss how the
one-minute series could continue.

Yours sincerely,

Observations Network Manager
