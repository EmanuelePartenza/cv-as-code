## C — Each position you have held

### Principal Keeper, Skerra Head Lighthouse

- C1. Role, organisation, where, when.
  > Principal Keeper, Northern Isles Lights Board, Skerra Head Lighthouse, Skerra. From April 2018, ongoing.
- C2. What the organisation does.
  > Runs the lighthouses, buoys and fog signals of the Northern Isles.
- C3. What you actually did.
  > I am the keeper: the light turns because I keep it turning. Daily operation of a first-order rotating light and the fog signal, on a rota with one relief keeper. I maintain the lens drive, the lamp changer and the standby generator myself; the Board's engineers come twice a year. I take the weather observations every three hours and send them to the met service. I plan the supply landings, four a year: inventory, the order, and the landing plan with the supply vessel's master, because the jetty only works in two wind directions. When something happens at sea below us I am the one on the VHF with the coastguard. I trained the relief keepers and wrote the station handbook because there wasn't one. I manage the station's budget for consumables and spares.
- C4. Numbers, where they are real.
  > Six years without an unplanned outage of the light, 2018 to 2024 (there was one in 2025, storm damage to the drive, 40 minutes; I'm not hiding it, it just ends the run). Fourteen incidents with the coastguard on the radio since 2019, three of them helicopter medical evacuations — those are exact, they're in the log. Eight observations a day. Four landings a year. Five relief keepers trained. The handbook is 40 pages. The budget is about £40,000 a year — an estimate, it moves with the diesel price.
- C5. Results.
  > The light kept turning. The Board renewed the manned-station status for Skerra in 2023 partly on the incident record; I don't have that in writing.
- C6. Anything you would rather not show.
  > Nothing to hide. I would not put the 2025 outage on a CV, but I'd answer if asked.
- C7. Documents.
  > The station logbook (I can copy an excerpt into the inbox), the handbook, the Board's annual inspection reports.
- Extra, since you ask about numbers: I think I cut the generator's diesel use by about a fifth when I re-scheduled the test runs in 2021, but I never worked the figure out properly. And in 2023 — I think 2023 — a kayaker got stuck below the cliffs at night and I talked the lifeboat in by radio. The log would confirm the date.
